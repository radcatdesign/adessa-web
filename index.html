The Adessa Quotient

See your mind in a new way.

Your AQ brings eight cognitive domains into one view. Each segment shows how your mind is working today, based on your own cognitive potential. The gaps reveal your untapped potential in every area.

Example AQ led by Attention

Four functional areas of your brain: Attention, Working Memory, Processing Speed, and Executive Function.

Four physiological states underneath: Mood, Mindfulness, Composure, and Resilience.

See your strongest cognitive domain (leading letter) and how much of your cognitive potential you’re using (digit).

Your results are measured against your own cognitive capacity. There are no “low” or “high” scores, only potential.

Unfilled segments aren’t a deficit, they reveal where you have unused cognitive capacity and steer your Activation Sessions.
