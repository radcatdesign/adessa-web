## How we measure use of this website

We measure how people use adessamind.com with our own analytics system. It runs on our website and our database. We don't use Google Analytics or other third-party analytics for this, and we don't sell or share this data.

**What we collect.** When you open a page we record the page address, the website that sent you (if any), campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source), your device type (phone, tablet or desktop), your browser and operating system names, your browser language, and your approximate location (country, region and city). We work out your approximate location from your IP address when the page loads and then discard the IP address. We don't store it. When you leave a page we record how long you actively used it and how far you scrolled. We also record some actions: clicks on email and phone links, file downloads, links to other websites, and form submissions. For forms we record which form was sent, never what you typed into it. To see which parts of a page get attention, we record where clicks and mouse movement happen, grouped into a coarse grid. These are counted in daily totals, not as recordings of your visit.

**No cookies.** Our analytics don't use cookies.

**Counting visitors.** To count how many people visit each day without storing anything on your device, our server combines your IP address and browser details with a random value that changes every day, and keeps only the result. We delete each day's random value when the day ends, so these counts can't be linked from one day to the next or traced back to you.

**Browser ID.** To understand return visits, we may store a random identifier in your browser's local storage (not a cookie). It contains nothing about you. We keep it for up to 13 months, after which a new one is created. We don't set it, and we don't keep any per-browser history, if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, or if your device is set to a time zone in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. For those visitors we only count daily totals.

**Linking to your email.** If you submit one of our forms (for example the waitlist) from a browser that has a browser ID, we may link that browser ID to the email address you gave us, so we can understand how people found us before they signed up.

**Who can see it.** Only Adessa staff, through a private dashboard that requires sign-in.

**How long we keep it.** Detailed records are deleted after about 400 days. We keep daily totals (for example, how many people viewed each page on a given day), which don't identify anyone.

**Your choices.** You can turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser, or clear your browser's local storage for adessamind.com, which removes the browser ID. To ask us to delete the records linked to your browser or email address, contact us at inquire@adessamind.com.

**Other tools.** Our forms and some marketing on this site are provided by HubSpot, which uses its own cookies under its own policy. See HubSpot's privacy policy for details.
