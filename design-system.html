Design System

Version 1.0 · October 2026 · For every new page

01

How Adessa should feel

Neural networks meet natural materials. Pages are warm, quiet and precise: lots of cream space, light serif type, tracked caps for anything you can tap, and imagery of light moving through texture. This page and the Figma file are the same system; if they ever disagree, Figma wins and this page gets updated.

Calm before clever

One idea per section. Let the type and the texture do the work; skip decoration that doesn’t carry meaning.

Warm, never clinical

Cream and stone instead of white and grey. Science is in the substance, not in the styling.

Precise where it counts

Numbers, dates and actions are set in tracked caps or Display figures so they read at a glance.

04

Spacing and radius

An 8px rhythm with a few large steps for breathing room. Sections sit 120px apart on desktop, 80px on tablet and 64px on phones (the ds-section class handles it). Corners are soft on images and panels, fully round on anything you can tap.

05

Imagery and texture

Light moving through fine strands, smoke and water. Warm sepia tones, soft focus, no hard edges. Use textures full-bleed behind heroes and as card images; only put type on them with the overlay nav, glass buttons and white text. People photography follows the same palette: natural light, eyes closed or looking away, calm.

The full icon set and symbol marks are on the Brand page in Figma. Icons are 1.25px line drawings on a 24px grid, colored with text-secondary.

07

Building a page

New pages start from the starter template, which uses every component above. Existing Webflow pages keep their own styles until they are rebuilt.

Design it in Figma

Start from the Components page. Use the Color, Layout and text styles only; anything else is a sign the system needs a new token.

Copy the starter

Duplicate templates/starter.html to the repo root, rename it, and swap the content block by block.

Push to main

Vercel deploys on every push. Use a branch for a preview link to share before it goes live.
