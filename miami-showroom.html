02AQ, understand your mind

For the first time, an actionable baseline for cognitive wellbeing.

AQ doesn’t just describe how your mind is performing today. It is designed to inform what you do next, and measure what changes.

  1. Measure

    EEG, HRV, rapid cognitive testing and check-in inputs.

  2. Interpret

    A weighted, traceable AQ across eight cognitive and affective domains.

  3. Activate

    A personalized intervention plan matching measured patterns to relevant modalities.

  4. Reassess

    Repeat measurement to examine change over time and refine programming.

03From neurotech to validated outcomes

The technologies exist. The opportunity is proving what works, for whom, and how to change minds.

The Institute for Advanced Consciousness Studies has proposed a structured experimental program at Adessa’s Miami showroom, beginning with a standardized active-control cohort and continuing through individual technology evaluations against that reference.

Benchmark

Establish a standardized reference using a 20-minute active control.

Test

Evaluate candidate interventions with consistent pre/post measurement.

Determine

Identify measurable responses and inform inclusion in Adessa’s protocols.

The study measures more than guest enjoyment. The proposed battery includes EEG, HRV, cognitive performance, self-reported mood and other psychological measures. Technology response profiles are judged against a common benchmark, with a go/no-go recommendation.

Validation questions

Measurement

Does the AQ-related battery reliably detect meaningful cognitive and affective differences and changes?

Intervention

Which technologies or protocols produce measurable changes beyond the active-control benchmark?

Personalization

Can those findings lead to defensible recommendations that improve the consumer experience and inform future programming?
